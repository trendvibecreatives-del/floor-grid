Settings

Ball mode

Turn on "Show motion" and throw a ball: red = what the camera sees moving, yellow ring = where it counted the hit. Auto threshold adjusts to how grainy the camera is. To set it by hand, untick Auto: missed throws → lower it, hits with no ball → raise it.

Touch delay: how long a hand or foot must stay on a tile before it counts. Raise it if tiles flicker or trigger when people just reach toward the wall.
